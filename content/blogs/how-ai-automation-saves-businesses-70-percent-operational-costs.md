## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between simple Zapier automation and Custom AI Agents?
Zapier handles rigid *if-this-then-that* triggers but fails when data is unstructured (e.g., answering nuanced customer emails or reading scanned PDFs). Custom AI Agents understand context, make reasoning decisions, handle edge cases, and call custom APIs dynamically.

### How long does it take to implement custom AI automation for my business?
A targeted custom AI workflow (such as automated customer support, invoice processing, or CRM lead enrichment) typically deploys in **1 to 3 weeks**, delivering measurable ROI within the first 30 days.

### Will AI automation replace my existing employees?
AI automation handles monotonous, low-leverage tasks so your core team can focus on high-value activities like closing enterprise deals, client relationships, and strategic growth.
