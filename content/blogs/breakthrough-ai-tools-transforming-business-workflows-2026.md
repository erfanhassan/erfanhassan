## 4. How to Invest in AI for Your Business Wisely

When budgeting for AI adoption in 2026, adhere to these three principles:

1. **Avoid "Wrapper" Subscriptions**: Avoid paying $50/seat/month for generic AI writing or generic productivity apps that lock your data into a proprietary walled garden.
2. **Invest in Owned Infrastructure**: Building custom API integrations and agent pipelines connected directly to your CRM and databases creates compounding equity and operational superiority.
3. **Prioritize Time-to-Value**: Focus first on automations that touch recurring revenue (lead response speed, churn prevention, contract turnaround).
